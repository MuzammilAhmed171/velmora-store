VELMORA SHOPPING GUIDE (Demo Digital Download)
============================================

Thank you for your purchase! This is a demo digital product included
with the Velmora starter package.

In a real store, this file would be your e-book, software license,
design template, or course material.

How digital downloads work in Velmora:
- After a customer buys a digital product, they get a secure,
  limited-use download link in their account (My Account > Downloads).
- Each link has a download limit set by the store admin.
- Admins upload files in Admin > Products by marking a product as digital.

Replace this file with your own digital products when you go live.
